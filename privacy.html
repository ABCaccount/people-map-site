Privacy Policy

People Map

Last updated: October 2, 2026

People Map is designed to let users see people around them anonymously. This Privacy Policy explains what information People Map collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Location Information

People Map collects precise location information to provide the app's core functionality, including displaying your anonymous presence on the map and showing anonymous users around you.

When you allow background location access, People Map may access your location while the app is not actively in use so that your map presence can remain up to date.

Birthday and Age

People Map requires users to be at least 18 years old. We collect your date of birth to determine your age and eligibility to use the service.

Your date of birth is associated with your anonymous People Map account. Your age may be included with your anonymous map presence to enable age-based filtering. Your date of birth is not displayed to other users.

Anonymous Account and Identifiers

People Map creates an anonymous account and identifier to operate the service. You are not required to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal identifiers to use the app.

The anonymous identifier may be associated with information necessary to provide People Map, such as your location, age, visibility status, and account settings.

Private Areas

People Map allows you to create Private Areas where your map presence is hidden. Private Area settings may be stored on your device to provide this functionality.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase a People Map subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. People Map does not receive or store your full payment card information.

People Map may receive information from Apple necessary to determine whether your subscription or purchase is active and to provide the corresponding features.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the anonymous people map.
  • Determine which anonymous users are relevant to the map experience.
  • Enable age filtering and Private Areas.
  • Maintain your visibility and app settings.
  • Provide subscription features.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and proper operation of the service.
  • Process account and data deletion requests.

Information Visible to Other Users

People Map is designed around anonymous map presence. Other users may be shown an approximate representation of your presence on the map and may be able to filter map presence based on age.

People Map does not provide other users with your name, email address, phone number, profile, or exact date of birth.

Service Providers

People Map relies on third-party service providers to operate the app. These may include:

  • Supabase, which provides backend infrastructure, authentication, database, and storage services.
  • Google Maps, which provides map functionality.
  • Apple, which provides App Store distribution and processes in-app purchases and subscriptions.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own privacy practices.

Advertising and Tracking

People Map does not use your precise location or anonymous identifier for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

People Map does not sell your personal information to data brokers.

Data Retention

People Map retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and reliability, comply with applicable legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your People Map account and associated data directly within the app by going to Settings → Subscription → Delete Account & Data.

Deleting your account removes your anonymous People Map account and associated active People Map data and clears locally stored People Map data from the app.

Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or technical backup purposes.

Deleting your People Map account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. App Store subscriptions are managed through Apple.

Location Permissions

You can manage People Map's location permissions through your device's iOS Settings. Disabling required location permissions may prevent some or all of People Map's functionality from working.

Children's Privacy

People Map is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. People Map does not permit users under 18 to use the service.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as People Map or its practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, People Map's privacy practices, or your data, contact us at contactteamabc@gmail.com.
