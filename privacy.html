Location Information

People Map collects precise location information to provide the app's core functionality, including displaying anonymous users on the map.

Anonymous Identifiers

People Map uses an anonymous identifier to distinguish users and maintain map functionality. This identifier is not intended to identify you by name, email address, phone number, or other real-world identity.

Advertising and Tracking

People Map does not use your precise location or anonymous user identifier for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking, and does not sell this information to data brokers.

Location Permissions

You can manage location permissions for People Map through your device's iOS Settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as People Map changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
